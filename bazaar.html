Rebranding Bazaar's logo

"Bazaar" is a local grocery store located downtown, specializing in a variety of Middle Eastern and Indian products. Their goal is to offer customers a warm and inviting shopping experience.

My Role

This was a solo college project where I led the design process from research and concept development to the final visual identity. I focused on redesigning Bazaar's logo and creating a cohesive brand direction that could extend across its online grocery service and new Ready-to-Eat offering.

Graphic Design Branding Solo Team

Inspiration

The inspiration came from Bazaar's Middle Eastern roots and its warm, welcoming shopping experience. I wanted the new identity to feel modern and approachable while still reflecting the brand's cultural character. The logo exploration focused on creating a simple, flexible mark that could work across the physical store, online grocery platform, and Ready-to-Eat products.

Challenges

  • Add new service (online website)
  • No color branding 
  • Keep same feeling for app 
  • Add the new Ready-to-Eat meal
  • Mostly focus on middle-east groceries
