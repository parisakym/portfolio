Designing Booklet

This college project focused on designing a multi-page booklet from concept to final layout. The goal was to create a visually engaging and well-structured publication that effectively organized content while maintaining a consistent visual identity. The project involved developing page layouts, establishing typography and grid systems, selecting imagery, and ensuring a clear reading experience. Through this process, I explored the principles of editorial design, visual hierarchy, and information organization to create a cohesive and polished final booklet.
