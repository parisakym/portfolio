Designing To clarify brand messaging

This project explores the redesign of Orso Coffee Shop's logo, focusing on how small visual changes can modernize a brand while preserving its recognizable identity. The goal was to create a bold, refined logo that feels more relevant to today's coffee audience.

My Role

This was a solo college case study where I led the project from concept to final logo design. I explored the brand's identity, developed the visual direction, and refined the logo to create a stronger, more modern identity.

Graphic Design Branding Solo team

Inspiration

My inspiration came from the bold, handcrafted feel of independent coffee shops and the warmth of coffee culture. I wanted to keep Orso's original bear concept while exploring stronger shapes, expressive brush textures, and a more confident visual style. The goal was to create something that felt familiar to existing customers, yet fresh and distinctive for a new audience.

Challenge

The main challenge was finding the right balance between familiarity and change. I wanted to modernize Orso's logo and give it a stronger personality without losing the recognizable bear-and-coffee concept that customers already associated with the brand.
