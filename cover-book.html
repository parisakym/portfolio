Redesigning Cover Book

Recover Cover Books was a college graphic design project focused on redesigning existing book covers. The goal was to explore how typography, imagery, composition, and visual style can transform a book's identity and create a stronger connection with its audience. I experimented with different design directions while keeping each cover connected to the story and overall character of the book.

My Role

Solo college project. I was responsible for the entire design process, including research, concept development, cover design, and final execution.
