Anatomy of Typographic

Understanding the anatomy of typography is crucial for UX/UI designers as it serves as a foundational framework for mastering visual hierarchy, utilizing typeface characteristics effectively, and harnessing font psychology. Typography anatomy encompasses elements such as arms, legs, spines, spikes, ears, shoulders, and tails, providing designers with essential knowledge to craft compelling designs.
