Designing Personal brand

I created my personal brand as a visual expression of my cultural roots and creative perspective. It brings together my Iranian background and my approach to design in a way that feels personal, distinctive, and authentic.

My Role

I managed the project from concept to final execution, including visual exploration, logo development, and brand direction. My goal was to build a cohesive system that communicates my personality and design approach.

Graphic Design Branding Solo team

Problem

I wanted to create a personal brand that felt more meaningful than a simple visual mark. The challenge was to translate my background, personality, and design philosophy into a modern and cohesive visual language while keeping it clear, versatile, and functional.

Inspiration

My inspiration came from the Persian language, my cultural roots, and my passion for visual communication. This led me to explore the connection between the English "P" and Persian "پ", two forms that represent different sides of my story while coming together as one visual concept.
