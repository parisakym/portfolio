Designing To create a more memorable impression

This project focused on rebranding a dance school to create a more engaging and trustworthy brand identity. The goal was to reflect the school's welcoming, inclusive environment while highlighting the confidence, self-discipline, and sense of community that dance can foster. Through a refreshed visual identity, the brand was repositioned to appeal to dancers of all ages and experience levels, helping strengthen customer trust, loyalty, and connection with the organization.

My Role

This rebranding project was completed as an individual college case study. I was responsible for the entire design process, including research, brand strategy, concept development, logo design, visual identity creation, and the final brand application.

Graphic Design Branding Solo team

Inspiration

The inspiration for this rebrand came from the movement, rhythm, and sense of confidence that dance brings to people's lives. I explored visual elements that conveyed energy, fluidity, and connection while reflecting the welcoming atmosphere of the school. The goal was to create a modern and approachable identity that celebrates self-expression, personal growth, and the joy of dancing.

Challenges

The main challenge was creating a brand identity that appealed to a broad audience while capturing the energy and inclusivity of the dance school. The new identity needed to feel welcoming to dancers of all ages and skill levels, while also communicating the school's values of confidence, self-discipline, growth, and community. Balancing professionalism with a fun and approachable personality was key to ensuring the brand resonated with both new and existing students.
