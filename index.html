I'm a UX/UI designer with a background in graphic design. I care about creating digital experiences that are both beautiful and meaningful, combining research, empathy and visual craft to design products people enjoy using.

My process starts with understanding the problem, talking to users, mapping flows, and sketching before I open Figma. From there I move through wireframes and prototypes to high-fidelity UI, testing along the way so decisions are backed by evidence, not just taste. I'm most drawn to projects where clear structure and a strong visual system can make something genuinely easier to use.
